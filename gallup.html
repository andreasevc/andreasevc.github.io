overview.json

23 colleagues, each ranked across all 34 Gallup CliftonStrengths talents. The 34 talents are grouped into four domains — Executing, Influencing, Relationship Building, and Strategic Thinking. Names are anonymised; each person is identified by a numeric user ID.

talent_frequency.bar

For each talent, how many of the 23 people have it inside their top 5 signature strengths. Bars are coloured by domain, sorted descending.

Executing Influencing Relationship Building Strategic Thinking

domain_balance.json

Out of all top-5 slots in the department, what share lands in each domain? The baseline is what we'd expect if the 34 talents were drawn uniformly.

pca.svg

Two-dimensional projection of each person's full 34-talent rank profile, computed via principal component analysis. Points that sit close together have similar strength profiles overall; distance reflects difference across all talents — not just top 5. Each dot is colored by its owner's dominant top-5 domain; my own dot (User33) is outlined.

Executing Influencing Relationship Building Strategic Thinking

Computed in-browser: each row is centered, the n×n Gram matrix is formed, and the top two eigenvectors are extracted by power iteration with deflation. The 2D coordinates are the corresponding singular-value-scaled scores. No external linear-algebra library.

coverage.log

Talents that no one in the department has in their top 5 — and top 10. Useful to flag when assembling a team for a project that needs those strengths.

by_team.md

Per-team collective strengths. For each unit, talents ranked by average rank across its members — lower average = stronger collective signal.

pair_picker.js

Pick two or three people to see their combined signature strengths, how the four domains split across the group, and which talents none of them have in their top 10 — useful when staffing an engagement and you want to check who's bringing what.

best_combinations.json

For one selected person, the top five most complementary 2-person staffing combinations — with the specific talents that complement each other inside each pairing. Pool is limited to operational team members; managers and the methodology owner (User01, User02, User04) are excluded. Filled chips mean both members have that talent in their own top 5.

top_complementary_pairs.csv

All 253 possible pairs ranked by how many unique talents appear in their combined top 5 (max 10 if no overlap). Filled chips mean both members have that talent in their own top 5. Use the arrows to scroll through the ranking.

roster.tsv

Each person's top five talents. My own row (User33) is highlighted.

Source data: anonymised departmental CliftonStrengths results. Analysis computed client-side in vanilla JavaScript — no external libraries.
